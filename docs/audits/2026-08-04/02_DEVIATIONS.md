# 02 — Deviations

**Addon:** Ka0s Consumable Master · **Prefix:** `CM-` · **Audit:** 2026-08-04 · **Standard:** **v2.17.1 (2026-08-03)**

**Standard provenance.** The playbook, the index and **all 24 section files** were fetched over the
network from `raw.githubusercontent.com/tusharsaxena/WowAddonStandards/master` and each verified
**byte-identical** against the clean local canonical checkout at HEAD `2141122` (`v2.17.1`) with
`diff` / `diff -r`. Nothing was reconstructed from memory; no section was left unassessed. Full
provenance table in `01_CURRENT_STATE.md`.

IDs are **stable**. `CM-01…CM-29` and `CM-31…CM-33` from the 2026-07-12 and 2026-07-18 runs are
resolved and are not re-listed (see "Closed since the last run" below). **CM-30** persists and keeps
its ID. New IDs continue from **CM-34**; retired IDs are never re-purposed. Every row's `file:line`
evidence is in `03_EVIDENCE.md`. Severity is the strongest marker the violated rule carries.

| **CM-39** | `performance-§2`, anti-pattern #43 | MUST | **The bracket does an `NS` lookup per call instead of a load-time upvalue.** `core/ConsumableMaster.lua:268` and `modules/MacroBar.lua:322` both do `local perf = KCM.Perf` **inside** the timed function, then `(perf and perf.on) and debugprofilestop()`.
