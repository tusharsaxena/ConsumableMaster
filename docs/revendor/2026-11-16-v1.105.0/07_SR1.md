## What the release moved here

One case went red, `SR-L1`'s anchor flip: the drag strip's fallback placement (used when
`MB.PlaceTooltipBeside` cannot read the strip) now attaches the tooltip **above** the strip
(`BOTTOM` on `TOP`) rather than below. The case in `tests/test_macrobar_chrome.lua` was re-pinned to
the new contract, renamed "falls back above the strip", and its every other assertion kept (UIParent
owner, `ANCHOR_NONE`, never the cursor, the same lines, shown). The two comments in
`modules/MacroBar.lua` that named the old fallback side were corrected.

## Title-bar pass (rulings 3 and 4)

Consumable Master draws **no title-bar controls of its own**, so the step is skipped:

- the settings panel is the library's shell (its header is a title, a divider and the Defaults
  button, with no icon controls);
- the debug console, copy window, perf panel, sim panel and skin picker are library windows, covered
  by `SR-L2` and `SR-L3` from the re-vendor alone;
- the macro bar's drag strip is the library's `DragHandle`. Its marks already carry the addon's
  tooltips (strip, `?` and the X's "Hide the macro bar"), placed beside the strip by
  `MB.PlaceTooltipBeside` (TP-CM-02). That hook never sits under the pointer, so ruling 2's concern
  does not apply to it. The marks' colors are painted by the library widget, which still lights
  them in `icon.hot` at this tag (`WidgetsDragHandle.lua`), so any change there belongs upstream;
- `core/SkinSetup.lua` declares no window header of its own.

No header-band work: this addon paints no `list.header.bg`.
